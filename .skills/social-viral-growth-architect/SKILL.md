## 4. Community Guerrilla Seeding & Micro-KOL Playbook

### 4.1 Reddit Organic Seeding Rules (Strict Anti-Spam Compliance)
- **Target Subreddits**: `r/rarepuppers`, `r/aww`, `r/corgi`, `r/frenchies`, `r/goldenretrievers`, `r/standardissuecat`.
- **Golden Rule**: **NEVER post a direct link in the main post submission**. Reddit algorithms and moderators instantly shadowban promotional links.
- **Execution**:
  1. Post title must focus 100% on the pet's character (e.g., *"My 12-year-old rescue finally got his presidential portrait done"*).
  2. Upload only the high-res 1080P PNG.
  3. Wait for organic comments asking *"What program did you use?"*
  4. Reply genuinely in the comments: *"It's a free web tool called ViviBon Cover! You just upload a face photo and it preserves their exact markings in editorial styles."*

### 4.2 Micro-Influencer Direct Gifting (10k – 50k Followers)
- **Why Micro-KOLs**: 6.7x higher engagement rate than mega-influencers, zero agency barriers, intense pet-parent pride.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Identify top 100 active Instagram/TikTok pet accounts across target breeds (Corgi, Frenchie, Shiba, Golden).
  2. Take their public profile photo, run it through ViviBon Cover with the matching signature template (`royal_monarch` or `ivy_prep`).
  3. Direct Message (DM) them the pristine 1080P poster:
     *"Hey! We’re obsessed with [Pet Name]. Made this magazine cover for him just for fun using our studio tool, thought you’d love it for your wall! No strings attached, keep being awesome ❤️"*
  4. **Expected Conversion**: 35%+ will voluntarily post it to their Instagram Story and tag the tool or bio link out of sheer excitement.
